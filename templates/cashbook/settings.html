{% trans "Privacy Policy" %}

{% trans "How Cashbook handles the information you add." %}

{% trans "Anmol Gupta built the Cash Book app as an Ad Supported app. This service is provided by Anmol Gupta and is intended for use as is."%}

{% trans "Information stored in Cashbook" %}

{% trans "Cashbook stores the information needed to provide your account and cashbook: your email address, your name if you add it, and your display preferences such as language, date, time, currency, and theme." %}

{% trans "The financial records you enter are stored with your account, including transaction account names and currencies, cash-in and cash-out amounts, categories, descriptions, and dates. Cashbook also stores the notes and calendar events you create." %}

{% trans "For sign-in and email verification, Cashbook stores a password hash rather than your readable password. Verification codes are stored as hashes and expire; temporary verification records also track expiry, resend time, and failed attempts." %}

{% trans "How information is used" %}

{% trans "Cashbook uses this information to authenticate you, save and display your records, calculate account balances and summaries, apply filters, provide calculators, generate transaction exports you request, and remember your preferences." %}

{% trans "Your email address is used for account sign-in and verification. Verification messages are sent through the email service configured by the operator of your Cashbook installation." %}

{% trans "Access and sharing" %}

{% trans "Cashbook associates records with your user account and shows them after you sign in. The app does not connect to your bank or process payments; transactions are the information you enter." %}

{% trans "Cashbook does not sell your personal information. The operator of the installation and its infrastructure or email providers may process information as needed to host the app, maintain its database, or deliver verification messages. Their handling depends on how that installation is configured." %}

{% trans "When you export transactions to Excel or PDF, you choose where to save the file and whether to share it. Anyone with access to an exported file may be able to read its contents." %}

{% trans "Storage, retention, and deletion" %}

{% trans "Information is stored in the database configured for the Cashbook installation. The standard development setup uses a local SQLite database. The site operator determines where a deployed database and its backups are stored and how long backups are retained." %}

{% trans "You can edit or delete records using the available Cashbook features. To ask for removal of account information that you cannot delete in the app, contact the person or organization that operates your Cashbook installation. Deleted information may remain in backups until those backups are replaced or removed." %}

{% trans "Keeping your information safe" %}

{% trans "Cashbook uses Django's authentication and password-hashing features and requires a signed-in account to access personal records. No online service can guarantee absolute security. The operator is responsible for securing the server, database, email configuration, backups, and network connection; a deployed site should use HTTPS." %}

{% trans "Policy changes and questions" %}

{% trans "This policy may be updated when Cashbook's features or data practices change. For questions about this policy or a particular installation, contact that installation's operator." %}
